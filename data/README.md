## Data structure
- **Fulldata.csv** contains all data from both sites and years 2024 and 2025
- **Site1.csv contains** data from Site 1 for years 2024 and 2025
- **Site2.csv** contains data from Site 2 from the year 2025
---
**ID** - 	unique identifier composed of a row and a column

**site** -  identification of site
- H = Hermanky - Site 1
- L = Lisice - Site 2

**clone** - identification of clone

**row** - number of row

**col** - number of column

**VS_24 / VS_25** - Visual score of the given ramets in year 24 / 25

**FR_24 / FR_25**	- Flushing ratio of the given ramets in year 24/25

**GCC_24 / GCC_25**	- Green chromatic coordinate in year 24/25

**RCC_24 / RCC_25**	- Red chromatic coordinate in year 24/25
